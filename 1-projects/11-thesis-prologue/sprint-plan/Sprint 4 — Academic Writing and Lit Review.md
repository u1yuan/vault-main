# Sprint 4 — Academic Writing and Lit Review

← [[Sprint 3 — Git and Reproducible Workflow]] · [[Phase 1 — Foundation]] · Next → [[Phase 2 — Track P1 and Math]]

**Maps to:** P1 — Academic writing and literature review (both tracks)

## Project: "Mini critical review"

Pick a **provisional thesis topic** (one sentence). Find **12 papers** (Scopus/IEEE/Google Scholar + Boolean search log). Zotero/Mendeley library. Output:

- 1-page research gap statement
- **3 synthesis paragraphs** (each integrates ≥3 papers around one argument — not summaries)
- IMRaD outline for a future thesis chapter 2

## Deliverables

- `docs/lit_review.pdf`
- `.bib` export
- Search strategy appendix

## Resources

- Graff & Birkenstein *They Say / I Say*
- Purdue OWL
- University library workshops
